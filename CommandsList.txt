# Blind AI Assistant - Voice Commands Reference Guide

> [!TIP]
> **Conversational Freedom**: You can speak naturally! Words like *"please"*, *"can you"*, *"could you please"*, *"hey assistant"*, *"tell me"*, *"check"*, and *"for me"* are automatically filtered out. Both ultra-short 1-word commands and full conversational sentences work identically.

---

## 1. Quick Daily Essentials (1-Word Triggers)

| Action | Quick Commands | Conversational Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | `time`, `clock` | *"What time is it?"*, *"Tell me the time please"* |
| **Date** | `date`, `today`, `day` | *"What is today's date?"*, *"What day is it today?"* |
| **Time & Date** | `time and date`, `date and time` | *"Tell me the date and time"* |
| **Battery** | `battery`, `charge`, `power` | *"Check my battery"*, *"How much battery is left?"* |
| **Torch / Light On** | `torch`, `light`, `flashlight` | *"Turn on flashlight"*, *"Switch on the light please"* |
| **Torch / Light Off** | `torch off`, `light off`, `flashlight off` | *"Turn off the flashlight"*, *"Switch off the light"* |
| **Weather** | `weather`, `forecast` | *"What's the weather today?"*, *"How's the weather?"* |
| **Wi-Fi / Network** | `wifi`, `internet`, `network` | *"Is Wi-Fi connected?"*, *"Check internet status"* |
| **Bluetooth** | `bluetooth`, `check bluetooth` | *"Is Bluetooth on?"* |
| **Storage** | `storage`, `memory` | *"How much storage do I have?"*, *"Check storage"* |
| **Device Info** | `device info`, `about phone` | *"What phone model is this?"* |
| **Voice Guide** | `commands`, `what can you do`, `help commands` | *"What commands can I say?"*, *"How to use"* |

## 3. Assistive Sight, Camera Viewfinder & Photo Vision

*When triggered, a small live viewfinder popup opens at the top of the screen and displays the actual captured camera image.*

| Vision Mode | Voice Commands | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Surroundings & Scene** | `describe`, `look`, `see`, `camera`, `what's around me`, `look around` | Opens viewfinder popup, captures frame, and describes the full scene |
| **Take Photo / Picture** | `take photo`, `take picture`, `capture image`, `photo`, `picture`, `snapshot` | Snaps a camera photograph and speaks what was captured |
| **Obstacles & Hazards** | `obstacles`, `hazards`, `is it safe`, `watch my step`, `any danger` | Detects steps, curbs, drop-offs, and tripping hazards |
| **Count Money / Cash** | `money`, `cash`, `currency`, `count money`, `how much money is this` | Identifies banknotes, bills, and coins with total amount |
| **Color Detector** | `color`, `what color`, `what color is this`, `match my clothes` | Identifies clothing or item colors for outfit matching |
| **OCR & Text Reading** | `read text`, `read label`, `read sign`, `what does this say`, `ocr` | Reads medicine boxes, street signs, food labels verbatim |
| **Document Reader** | `read document`, `read mail`, `read letter`, `read paper`, `document` | Reads letters, receipts, and bills top-to-bottom |
| **Item Identifier** | `what is this`, `product`, `medicine`, `what am i holding` | Identifies household goods, groceries, and medicine packaging |
| **Object Finder** | `where is my <item>`, `find my keys`, `find my glasses` | Locates specific items using clock-face directions |
| **Dismiss Camera** | `close camera`, `dismiss camera`, `hide camera` | Closes and dismisses the camera popup viewfinder |
